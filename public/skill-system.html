ROADMAP

Phases overlap on purpose. Week numbers assume the heavier 3-hour-a-day schedule (about 19 hours a week). On 2 hours a day, stretch every timeline by roughly 40–50%, or drop the specialisation phase. The 24-month path is the complete system.

SKILLS

Six tracks from the dependency map. Arrows mean “needed before”. Optional steps are dashed. Cut branches before you cut the spine: Python/pandas → SQL → statistics → classical ML and evaluation → two end-to-end projects → deployment.

SKILL MATRIX

Classification is for a Data Scientist targeting product companies, GCCs and analytics-heavy firms. 17 MUST, 15 SHOULD, 3 NICE. Job-ready means you can do it unaided at a junior level and defend the choice.

PROJECTS

Every project answers a decision, uses realistic data, validates the way it will be deployed, beats a baseline, and ends in a written memo. From Project 4 onward, a stranger can run it in one command.

GATES

Take each gate cold: no notes, only official documentation, a timer on. If you fail, you do not skip. Spend 1–2 weeks on the failing skill and retest on a new problem set.
